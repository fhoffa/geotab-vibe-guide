## Safety, Coaching & Driver Behavior

**FleetShield AI** 🏆 — [github.com/klickgenai/geotab-hackathon](https://github.com/klickgenai/geotab-hackathon) · [Video](https://youtu.be/bb52r3x8EJw) · [Winner interview](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G3A8PjtovN8)

Voice AI operator assistant + autonomous mission orchestration. The operator asks Tasha to run a coaching sweep — the system scores every driver against their 90-day baseline, updates their portal, and places real Twilio phone calls to flagged drivers. 17 Claude agent tools, 9 insurance/safety scoring engines, 736-line CLAUDE.md.

---

**ClearSkies** — [github.com/reubenfrith/clearskies](https://github.com/reubenfrith/clearskies) · [Video](https://youtu.be/f5crxOlPiRA)

Weather-aware OSHA work-hold system for construction fleets. Knows exactly which vehicles are on each site when a storm hits, issues holds, and generates audit-ready compliance records. Solves a $5B/year problem in US construction.

---

**fleet-voice-alerts** — [github.com/pmetis/fleet-voice-alerts](https://github.com/pmetis/fleet-voice-alerts) · [Video](https://youtu.be/uCkQ6jYy4fI)

Detects fleet exceptions, enriches them with ACE, and calls supervisors directly via Twilio — the supervisor can ask follow-up questions by voice. Built for remote Latin American fleet operations where voice still reaches when data connectivity doesn't.

**GeoSafe** — [github.com/ribbeon/public-2026-geotab](https://github.com/ribbeon/public-2026-geotab) · [Video](https://share.descript.com/view/jmHeARJ7EKE)

Geofence intelligence platform. AI suggests zone boundaries based on where vehicles actually stop, combined with a conversational assistant for fleet queries.

**GEOPulse** — [github.com/crypticsaiyan/GEOPulse](https://github.com/crypticsaiyan/GEOPulse) · [Video](https://youtu.be/pn5sR1MiuvQ)

**Geoff** — [github.com/robertlagrasse/geotab-geoff](https://github.com/robertlagrasse/geotab-geoff) · [Video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-KtFKp8wk-M)
